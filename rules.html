CLUB RULES

These aren't suggestions. These are the lines you don't cross if you wear the name.

Brotherhood

Members support each other at all times.

Internal issues stay inside the club.

Disrespect toward patched members will not be tolerated.

Loyalty

Loyalty to the club comes before personal profit.

Snitching, leaking information, or working against the MC is forbidden.

Respect

Respect higher ranks and members.

Respect allied organizations.

Respect club property and club rules.

01

GENERAL RULES

—

Respect the chain of command at all times. Orders from higher ranks must be followed.

—

Club meetings are mandatory unless excused by the 3 shot callers.

—

No interrupting People during meetings.

—

No trolling or immature behavior.

—

The patch must always be respected. Don't wear your patch when fighting.

—

Never wear another club's colours.

—

Do not steal from or disrespect club property.

02

PROSPECT RULES

—

Prospects must respect all patched members.

—

Prospects speak when spoken to during formal meetings.

—

Prospects may be tasked with supply runs, security, bike maintenance, and club duties.

—

Complaining about prospect duties may result in removal.

—

Patches are earned through loyalty, trust, activity, and good mana.

03

CRIMINAL ACTIVITY RULES

—

Criminal activity must always have proper RP reasoning.

—

No random shootings or pointless violence.

—

Police interactions must stay realistic.

—

Members must value their life and other members life's during all situations.

—

Wars with rival groups require approval from 2 of the shot callers (Pres, Vp or Sgt of arms)

—

Members cannot start major conflicts without permission.

—

Civilians should not be harmed without reasoning.

04

MOTORCYCLE RULES

—

Members should ride motorcycles during official club activities whenever possible.

—

Club-style bikes, cruisers, and Harleys are preferred.

—

Follow Road Captain instructions during rides.

—

Maintain proper formation during convoys.

—

No reckless riding during official convoys.

—

Always wear your patch during convoys.

05

COMMUNICATION RULES

—

Keep radio communications clear when asked.

—

Do not yell over others during situations.

—

Club information stays private.

—

Do not leak plans, stash locations, or club operations.

—

Our radio is 23. If leaked black nation members will be punished.

06

PUNISHMENTS

MINOR PUNISHMENTS
  • Warnings
  • Extra duties
  • Fines
MAJOR PUNISHMENTS
  • Demotion
  • Patch removal
  • Blacklisting

All members must follow Beehive RP server rules and set the highest standard of roleplay. Breaking server rules reflects badly on the entire club and may put the organization and your position in the org at risk.
