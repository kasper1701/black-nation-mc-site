ABOUT BLACK NATION MC

Black Nation started on Oneal’s farm, where Koro and the other koro, Hema Tane, were holding up a house. During the job, Hema Tane started yelling “Black Nation”, and the name ended up sticking.

Not long after, a group of the nephs started linking up more often. What started as a few mates hanging out slowly turned into a solid group built around loyalty, trust, and having each other’s backs.

As time went on, the group grew and Black Nation became more than just a name. While things have changed over time, the idea behind it has stayed the same good people, strong connections, and staying loyal to the circle.
