Vault Credential Generator

Everything here runs locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. Type the password you want, click Generate, then paste the two values it gives you into js/vault.js, replacing SALT_HEX and HASH_HEX. Do not paste the plain password anywhere in vault.js — only these two generated values.

Keep this file off your live server if you can (or at least don't link to it) — it's not a secret tool, but there's no reason to leave it lying around publicly. A long, random passphrase is worth far more here than any amount of hashing — "correct horse battery staple" style beats a short word every time.
