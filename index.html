BLACK NATION INGAME NEWS

With the recent announcement regarding the removal of Gang RP, Black Nation as a gang can no longer exist within the server. This means all Black Nation branding, references and wording must be removed, and we cannot claim to be Black Nation going forward.

That said, this does not mean we have to stop using our characters or stop roleplaying together. We can still do RP and create scenes as a crew.

The only change is that we cannot represent ourselves as Black Nation or operate as a gang under that name. Let's continue creating good RP together while respecting the new server rules.
