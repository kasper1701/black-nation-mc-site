RANK STRUCTURE

Chain of command is everything. You know your place, you respect the patch above you, and you back the patch beside you.

President

Final say on all club matters. Controls wars, alliances, promotions, and punishments.

Vice President

Second in command. Runs the club when the President is unavailable.

Sergeant At Arms

Handles security and discipline. Maintains order during meetings and operations.

Treasurer

Manages club money, supplies, and assets.

Road Captain

Organizes rides and convoys. Leads club movement during runs and events.

Members

Full members of Black Nation MC. Expected to remain active and loyal.

Prospects

Trial members proving loyalty and dedication. Must respect all patched members.

PATH TO THE PATCH

  1. Earn trust. Show up. Stay solid.
  2. Prospect. Do the work nobody sees.
  3. Prove loyalty when it counts.
  4. Get patched. Carry the name properly.
